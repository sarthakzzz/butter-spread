BUTTER SPREAD: READ ME FIRST (MAC)
==================================

Thanks for picking up Butter Spread! Installing takes about a minute.


A QUICK, HONEST NOTE FIRST
--------------------------
We're a tiny studio and our Apple signing is still being set up.
Until it's done, macOS doesn't recognise us yet and may say it
"can't check" the installer. The file is exactly what we built.
Only download Butter Spread from pluginsforbreakfast.com.


STEP 1: OPEN THE INSTALLER
--------------------------
Double-click Butter-Spread-1.0.0-mac.pkg.

If macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it:
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK"). Don't move it to the Bin.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down. Next to the message about Butter Spread,
     click "Open Anyway", then enter your Mac password.
  4. Click "Open" when asked again.

On older macOS (before Sequoia) you can also right-click (or
Control-click) the .pkg, choose "Open", then click "Open".


STEP 2: FOLLOW THE INSTALLER
----------------------------
Click Continue until it says the installation was successful.
Butter Spread goes in as an Audio Unit (AU) and a VST3.


STEP 3: RESCAN IN YOUR DAW
--------------------------
  Logic Pro:    Restart Logic. If it doesn't show up, open
                Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager,
                select Butter Spread and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".
  Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan.
  Others:       look for "Plugin Manager" or "Rescan plugins",
                or simply restart the DAW.

You'll find it under "Plugins for Breakfast".


STILL STUCK?
------------
Email chef@pluginsforbreakfast.com and tell us your macOS version
and DAW. We usually reply within a day.
