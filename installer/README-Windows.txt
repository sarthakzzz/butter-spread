HOW TO INSTALL ON WINDOWS
=========================

Thank you for your delicious purchase! Installing takes about a minute.


A QUICK, HONEST NOTE FIRST
--------------------------
We're a tiny studio. Windows code-signing certificates
cost a few hundred dollars a year, and we'd rather spend that once
our plugins are paying for themselves. So for now our Windows files
are not signed.

That means Windows doesn't recognise us yet and may show a warning.
The files are exactly what we built, and we'll sign them as soon as
sales allow. Until then, here's how to install safely.

Please only download our plugins from our own website or store.
If you got them anywhere else, delete them and download a fresh copy.


STEP 1: UNZIP
-------------
Right-click the downloaded .zip and choose "Extract All".


STEP 2: RUN SETUP
-----------------
Double-click the Setup file. If a blue box says
"Windows protected your PC":
1. Click "More info".
2. Click "Run anyway".

This box appears for any new, unsigned app. It is not a virus warning.
Setup puts everything in the right place for you.


STEP 3: RESCAN IN YOUR DAW
--------------------------
Open your DAW and ask it to look for new plugins:

  Ableton Live:  Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins.
                 Turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then Rescan.
  FL Studio:     Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.
  Cubase/Nuendo: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager > Rescan (or restart Cubase).
  Studio One:    Studio One > Options > Locations > VST Plug-Ins > Apply,
                 then restart.
  Reaper:        Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan.
  Other DAWs:    look for "Plugin Manager" or "Rescan plugins",
                 or simply restart the DAW.

You'll find our plugins under "Plugins for Breakfast".


STILL STUCK?
------------
- Make sure you're using a 64-bit DAW (our plugins are 64-bit only).
- If your antivirus moved a file to quarantine, please email us
  rather than switching your protection off. We'll help.

Email: chef@pluginsforbreakfast.com
We usually reply within a day or two.

Enjoy breakfast!
